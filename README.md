### 🧭 About me

I build **autonomous robots**, from indoor mobile robots to **underwater vehicles**, with a focus on
simulation-first development: model the physics, close the control loop, then move to hardware.
I'm looking for a **research role** where I can work with experts and push what AI and robotics can do.

*Torpedo AUV simulation on ROS 2 Humble + Gazebo Fortress*

- ⚓ Trimmed buoyancy with a real water surface & seabed
- 🌀 Fossen hydrodynamics: added mass + linear/quadratic damping
- 🐟 Propeller thruster, rudder & elevator fins with lift/drag
